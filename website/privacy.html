Your photos

We do not collect your photos. Images are processed entirely on your device — background removal, brush edits, and exports never leave your phone unless you choose to save or share them yourself.

Advertising

KatharErase is ad-supported on the free tier. We use Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers for ad personalization. On iOS, we request your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before any personalized tracking occurs — you can decline, and non-personalized ads will still be shown. See Google's own privacy policy for details on how AdMob handles data: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

The optional $0.99/month ad-free subscription is processed entirely through Apple's App Store or Google Play — we do not receive or store your payment information.

No account required

KatharErase does not require an account, and we do not operate a backend server that stores your data. Export history and settings are stored locally on your device only.
